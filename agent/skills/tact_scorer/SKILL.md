# Skill: Intercultural Tact & Fluency Scorer

## Scoring Dimensions
1. **Language Fluency Score (0 - 100):**
   - Grammatical clarity, natural sentence flow, vocabulary richness, clarity of intent.
2. **Cultural Tact Score (0 - 100):**
   - High vs. Low Context bridging: Did the student state their needs clearly without being confrontational?
   - Psychological Safety & Face-saving: Did the message preserve mutual respect?
   - Solution Orientation: Did the student offer an actionable alternative?

## Grading Rubric
* **90 - 100 (Mastery):** Clear, empathetic, diplomatic, proposes concrete collaborative steps.
* **75 - 89 (Effective):** Polite and clear, minor phrasing could be more diplomatic.
* **50 - 74 (Needs Tuning):** Either too blunt (risks defensiveness) or too timid/vague (risks missed deadlines).
* **< 50 (High Friction):** Aggressive, accusatory, or completely uncooperative.
